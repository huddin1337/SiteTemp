Weaponize the Resume 📄

Translate the foundation into a recruiter-friendly artifact. Optimized for the 6–10 second scan: target role visible, tools named early, relevant experience first.

📌 Resume Template Source

Free, clean, ATS-friendly template to start from:

→ uniqcv.com/free

Single-column layout · Standard headers · No graphics that break parsers.

✍️ Professional Summary

Short. One brief sentence covering:

  • The cybersecurity job you're targeting
  • Training & certifications earned
  • Practical projects / lab experience

📎 Reference screenshot for tone & length.

💼 Professional Experience

  • Tie experience directly to the job applied for
  • Keep bullets brief and relatable
  • Reframe retail wins → security-relevant skills
  • Lead with impact, not job duties

🎯 Recruiter Scan Test

  • Target role visible under the name
  • Tools/tech named in first 2–3 lines of summary
  • Security section before unrelated history
  • Bold-prefix skill categories as keyword anchors
